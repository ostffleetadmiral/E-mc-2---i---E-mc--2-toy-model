\begin{abstract}
We present a computational mathematical framework based on the structural axiom $0^0 = i$, the octonion algebra $\mathbb{O}$, the exceptional Jordan algebra $J_3(\mathbb{O})$, and the $E_8$ root system. The framework generates a dimensional chain $0^0 = i \to \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{H} \to \mathbb{O} \to 8D \to 6D$ interior, with a $1/8$ consciousness aperture arising from the ratio $421/3375 = 1/8 - 7/27000$. All arithmetic is verified using Q128.128 fixed-point arithmetic (256-bit signed, round-to-nearest-even multiplication, error bound $\leq 2^{-129}$), ensuring bit-exact reproducibility without IEEE-754 floating-point drift. We classify 36 framework claims by verification level: 16 as PROVEN (exact mathematics), 10 as INTERPRETATION (framework labeling), 3 as NUMEROLOGY, 4 as CONSTRUCTION, and 3 as UNVERIFIED. A literature review identifies 24 independent published references, with 5 claims independently verified and 13 independently reinforced by external research groups. The framework is designated a ``toy-model''---a play on the seriousness of the approach---and we explicitly distinguish established mathematics from speculative physical interpretation.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

This paper presents a computational framework that connects the octonion algebra $\mathbb{O}$, the exceptional Jordan algebra $J_3(\mathbb{O})$, and the $E_8$ root system through a generative chain rooted in the structural axiom $0^0 = i$. The framework is implemented in Zig using Q128.128 fixed-point arithmetic \cite{ramsey2026q128}, with quantum witnesses in Q\# and floating-point validation in isolated sidecar modules. Companion papers describe the Q128.128 arithmetic engine \cite{ramsey2026q128}, codon routing \cite{ramsey2026codon}, and the Neuraleak sentience testing framework \cite{ramsey2026neuraleak} in detail.

The designation ``toy-model'' is deliberate: it signals both the exploratory nature of the work and a play on the seriousness of the mathematical structures involved. The octonions, Jordan algebras, and $E_8$ are well-established mathematics. The framework's contribution is to connect them through a specific generative chain and to verify all arithmetic identities computationally with deterministic fixed-point arithmetic.

\subsection{Scientific Scope}

We explicitly distinguish:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{PROVEN}: Exact mathematics, independently verifiable (16 claims).
\item \textbf{INTERPRETATION}: Framework labeling applied to mathematical facts (10 claims).
\item \textbf{NUMEROLOGY}: Small-number coincidences (3 claims).
\item \textbf{CONSTRUCTION}: Objects built to match a target, not derived (4 claims).

Numerical correspondences alone do not constitute physical derivations. A valid physical derivation requires dimensional consistency, an independent model, parameter independence, generative equations, and predictive validation.

\begin{equation}

\end{equation}
This follows from $2^3 - 1 = 7$, which is a tautological identity.

\subsection{The 421 Identity}

\begin{theorem}[421 Identity]
\begin{equation}
421 = \frac{15^3 - 7}{8} = \frac{3375 - 7}{8} = \frac{3368}{8} = 421
\end{equation}
Furthermore:
\begin{equation}
\frac{421}{3375} = \frac{1}{8} - \frac{7}{27000}

\begin{proof}
Cross-multiply: $421 \times 27000 = 11{,}367{,}000$ and $3375 \times (3375 - 7) = 3375 \times 3368 = 11{,}367{,}000$. $\square$
\end{proof}

The ratio $421/3375 \approx 0.12474 \approx 1/8 = 0.125$ is interpreted as a ``consciousness aperture''---the fraction of the $15^3$ lattice that is conscious. The deviation from exactly $1/8$ is $7/27000 \approx 0.000259$.
