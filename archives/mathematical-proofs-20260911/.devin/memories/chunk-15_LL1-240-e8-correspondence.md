## Mathematical Structure

### Axiom
$$ L(L+1) = 240 $$

### Factorization
$$ L^2 + L - 240 = 0 $$
$$ (L - 15)(L + 16) = 0 $$

### Solution
$$ \boxed{L = 15, \quad L + 1 = 16} $$

### E8 Correspondence
$$ \boxed{15 \times 16 = 240} $$
$$ \boxed{|E_8 \text{ roots}| = 240} $$

Two independent routes to the same number 240.

## Key Results

1. L = 15 and L+1 = 16 are derived exactly from the axiom L(L+1) = 240
2. The number 240 appears through two completely independent routes:
   - The L/L+1 construction: 15 × 16 = 240
   - The canonical E8 root system: |E8 roots| = 240
3. This is an **exact numerical correspondence** — much stronger than finding 240 in a numerical fit
4. **Important caveat**: This does NOT by itself prove the lattice is an E8 lattice. An actual embedding/metric is needed.
